Glaucoma

A group of eye conditions that can damage the optic nerve, often linked to increased pressure inside the eye.

Diabetic Retinopathy

A diabetes complication that affects eyes by damaging blood vessels of the light-sensitive tissue at the back.

Macular Degeneration

A medical condition which may result in blurred or no vision in the center of the visual field.

Cataracts

Clouding of the normally clear lens of your eye, causing vision to become misty or blurred over time.
